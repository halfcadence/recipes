## Ingredients

### Gâteau

| | |
|---|---|
| 132g | granulated sugar |
| 1 | vanilla bean (or 8g vanilla extract) |
| 2 | large eggs |
| 71g | all-purpose flour |
| 4g | baking powder |
| 3g | table salt |
| 1020–1135g | Granny Smith apples (4–5), peeled |
| 13g | apple jelly, melted |

### Brown Sugar–Cinnamon Whipped Cream

| | |
|---|---|
| 231g | heavy cream, cold |
| 27g | dark brown sugar, packed |
| 0.5g | ground cinnamon |

## Steps

1. Split the vanilla bean lengthwise and scrape the seeds into the sugar in a large bowl. Add the eggs and whisk until smooth. Whisk in the flour, baking powder, and salt until smooth. The batter will be thick.

2. Slice the apples on a mandoline 2.5mm thick: slice one side down to the core, turn the apple 90° and slice to the core, then do the remaining 2 sides. Discard the cores. Reserve 18 well-shaped slices for the top. Fold the rest into the batter to coat and set aside for 15 minutes.

3. Meanwhile, set oven racks in the middle and lower-middle positions. Put a foil- or parchment-lined rimmed baking sheet on the lower rack to catch drips and heat the oven to 350°F. Grease an 8½ × 4½-inch metal loaf pan with vegetable oil spray. Line it with an 8 × 12-inch sheet of parchment, smoothed flush to the sides with the excess hanging over the long sides. Lightly grease the parchment.

4. Stir the apples, breaking up clumps, until every surface is lightly coated. The batter will have loosened.

5. Lift the apples into the pan a slotted-spoonful at a time, letting excess batter drain back into the bowl. Keep most slices flat (some folding is fine) and level each addition with the back of the spoon. Continue until all the apples are in the pan and about 40g of batter remains.

6. Dip one side of each reserved slice in the remaining batter and shingle them, batter side down, in 6 rows of 3 on top. Press firmly with the back of the spoon to level. Spread any leftover batter over the top and smooth it.

7. Bake on the middle rack at 350°F for 1½–1¾ hours, rotating the pan halfway, until the top is well browned and a skewer slides easily through the apples. Transfer to a wire rack.

8. Run a knife along the short sides of the pan. Brush the top with the melted apple jelly. Cool in the pan for 1½ hours; the cake will deflate. Lift it out by the parchment onto the rack and cool completely, at least 2 hours more.

9. Combine the cream, brown sugar, and cinnamon in a stand mixer fitted with the whisk. Whip on medium-low until foamy, about 1 minute, then on high to soft peaks, 1–3 minutes.

10. Slice the cake with a sharp chef's knife and serve with the cream.

## Analysis

<div class="analysis" markdown="1">

Total batter weight: **310g** (apples and jelly excluded)

| Component | Amount | % |
|---|---|---|
| Structure solids (flour + baking powder) | 75g | 24.2% |
| Fat (yolk) | 10g | 3.2% |
| Sugar | 132g | 42.6% |
| Liquid (egg water) | 78g | 25.2% |
| Egg/protein | 12g | 3.9% |

**Less a cake batter than a sweet glue for 3.5× its weight in apples, which are ~78% of the cake.** Against a light sponge, structure (24%) is in range, but sugar is high (43% vs 20–30%) and liquid low (25% vs 30–40%): the sugar pulls juice from the slices during the 15-minute rest, and that juice loosens the batter into a thin coat. Fat is only the yolks' (3%, below the sponge's 5–10%), so there is no crumb; the apple layers are the texture.

## Notes

- **Check the slice thickness before slicing everything.** Five stacked slices should measure 13mm (½ inch); adjust the mandoline until they do. Wear a cut-resistant glove.
- **Pan:** metal, 8½ × 4½ inches (22 × 11cm), with sides at least 2¾ inches (7cm) tall.
- Use Granny Smith. The tartness balances a batter that is 43% sugar; don't substitute a sweeter apple.
- No vanilla bean: whisk 8g vanilla extract in with the eggs.
- Maple syrup or honey can replace the apple jelly glaze, with a more distinct flavor.
- Gluten-free: King Arthur Measure for Measure flour swaps 1:1 for the all-purpose flour.
- Plan about 6 hours, 3½ of them cooling.
- Whipping technique, stages, and rescue: [Whipped Cream]({{ '/r/whipped-cream' | relative_url }}).
