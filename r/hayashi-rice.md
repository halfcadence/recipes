## Ingredients

| | |
|---|---|
| 10g | neutral oil |
| 2 | onions, small dice |
| 180g | mushrooms, small dice |
| 454g | ground beef |
| per box | water |
| 1 box | hayashi rice roux (S&B Tasty Hayashi Rice, 160g) |
| | Cooked short-grain rice, to serve |

## Steps

1. Heat oil in a large pot over medium. Add onions and mushrooms and cook, stirring occasionally, until soft and the mushroom liquid has cooked off, 12–15 minutes.

2. Raise heat to medium-high. Add beef and cook, breaking it up, until browned with no pink left, 5–7 minutes. Spoon off excess fat.

3. Add water to the amount on the roux box. Bring to a boil.

4. Turn off the heat. Break the roux into the pot and stir until dissolved.

5. Simmer on low, stirring often, until thick, about 10 minutes.

6. Serve over rice.

<div class="notes" markdown="1">
## Notes

- Any hayashi roux works. Use the water amount from whichever box you buy.
- The roux clumps in boiling liquid. Always add it off the heat.
- The sauce scorches on the bottom once the roux is in. Scrape the base when you stir.
- It thickens overnight. Reheat with a splash of water.

</div>
