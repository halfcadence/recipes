## Ingredients

| | |
|---|---|
| 450g | shrimp, cooked and peeled (see steps) |
| 240g | Clamato (or tomato juice + 30g clam juice) |
| 40g | ketchup |
| 60g | lime juice, fresh |
| 15g | Worcestershire sauce |
| 10g | Salsa Huichol or Valentina |
| 4g | Diamond Crystal kosher salt |

### Folded In

| | |
|---|---|
| 90g | white onion, minced fine |
| 100g | roma tomato, seeded, small-diced |
| 20g | cilantro, chopped |
| 2 | serrano chiles, minced |

### To Serve

| | |
|---|---|
| 1 | avocado, cut into wedges |
| 1 | lime, cut into wedges |
| | Saladitas or tostadas |

## Steps

1. Poach the shrimp shell on and peel them, following [Shrimp Cocktail]({{ '/r/shrimp-cocktail' | relative_url }}) steps 1 through 7.

2. Whisk the Clamato, ketchup, lime juice, Worcestershire, hot sauce, and salt. Chill 30 minutes.

3. Taste and adjust — more lime for sharpness, more hot sauce for heat, more salt for depth. It should taste bracing on its own.

4. Cut the shrimp in half crosswise if they're 21 count or larger. Leave smaller ones whole.

5. Fold in the shrimp, onion, tomato, cilantro, and serrano. Chill at least 1 hour, up to 4.

6. Spoon into tall goblets. Lay avocado wedges on top. Serve with lime wedges and saltines or tostadas.

## Notes

- Clamato carries the umami: it is tomato juice with clam broth and MSG. For a shrimpier base, simmer the shells in the strained poaching liquid 15 minutes, chill, and swap it in for up to half the Clamato.
- It should be soupy enough to eat with a spoon. Thin with more Clamato if it tightens up in the fridge.
- No horseradish and no Old Bay. The lime and the Huichol do that job.
- Better made 2 hours ahead. Past 4 the onion loses its edge.
- Clamato is standard in Sinaloa. Tomato juice plus a splash of clam juice is close; V8 is too vegetal.
- Add the avocado at the table, not into the mix — folded in, it breaks down and clouds the liquid.
- Keeps 2 days refrigerated, though the vegetables soften. Don't freeze.
- Aguachile is a different dish — raw shrimp cured in lime with chile, no tomato. Don't use this base for it.
</div>
