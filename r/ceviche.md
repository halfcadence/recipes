## Ingredients

| | |
|---|---|
| 450g | tilapia or other lean white fish, skinless and boneless, frozen and thawed (see notes) |
| 3g | Diamond Crystal kosher salt |
| 240g | lime juice, fresh (about 8 limes), or enough to cover |
| 30g | orange juice, fresh — bitter orange if you can find it |
| 2g | lime zest |
| 1g | sugar |
| 1–2 | serrano chiles, seeded and minced |
| 1 | red onion (about 150g), sliced paper-thin into half-moons |
| 20g | cilantro, leaves and tender stems, chopped |

## Steps

1. Cut the fish into ½-inch cubes. Smaller pieces cure faster.

2. Toss the fish with the salt in a glass, ceramic, or stainless container.

3. Pour over the lime juice and orange juice. Add the onion, serrano, zest, and sugar. Press everything under the juice; add more lime juice if any fish is exposed.

4. Cover and refrigerate 4 hours, until the fish is opaque white and firm through the center. Cut a cube open to check.

5. Stir in the cilantro. Taste and adjust the salt.

6. Serve cold, with tortilla chips or boiled sweet potato.

<div class="notes" markdown="1">
## Notes

- Lime juice does not kill parasites. Use fish that has been frozen to −4°F for 7 days, then thawed — most commercially frozen fish already has been.
- This is the long cure: firm and opaque all the way through. Check from 2 hours for a softer center. For Peruvian-style, tender and barely set inside, cure only a few minutes and serve immediately.
- White onion is a straight swap — crisper and a little sharper, and it stays white. Yellow works too; soak the slices in ice water 10 minutes first, or use a sweet yellow (Walla Walla, Vidalia). Red onion is the only one that turns pink in the lime.
- Other fish: corvina, sea bass, grouper, flounder, or shrimp. Avoid oily fish — salmon, mackerel, sardines, tuna, trout — for a cure this long.
- For real heat, halve 3–4 habaneros or scotch bonnets (ají chombo in Panama), seed them, and cure them in the juice with the fish. Pull them out before serving.
- Stainless steel is fine for the container and the spoon. Avoid aluminum, copper, and cast iron, which react with the lime and taste metallic.
- Keeps 48 hours refrigerated, airtight. Don't freeze.
